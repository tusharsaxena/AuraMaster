## The title-bar pass (rulings 3 and 4)

This addon draws one window of its own with controls on a title band, the **preset picker**
(`settings/PresetPicker.lua`, `AuraMasterPresetPicker`). The settings panel, the debug console, the
copy, perf and sim windows and the skin picker are the library's. The HUD containers' drag strips are
the library's `W.DragHandle` too, with its own marks and their tooltips.

| Window | Title-bar control | Tooltip | Rest | Hover |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Preset picker | close X (`NS.MakeCloseButton`, Core's close with the owner) | "Close" (`Tooltip.CloseTip`), above it through `AttachTitleTip` / `PlaceAttached`, in the owner's `gtip.title` | `close.rest`, which is `icon.tint` in every skin | `close.hot` |

The lock and test-mode buttons sit in the window's body, not on its title band. They are text buttons
whose labels carry the state, so the pass leaves them alone.

**What changed in code: nothing.** The close already went through Core's `MakeCloseButton` with the
owner, so the re-cut library gives it its tooltip and colors. `W.HeaderButtons` was not adopted: the
title band holds one control, and adopting the strip (with its settings mark) is owed at adoption under
WowAddonStandards v2.113.0, not at this re-vendor.

**Pinned by** `tests/test_presetpicker_titlebar.lua` (5 cases): under Ka0s Default and Azeroth, every
title-bar control shows a non-empty tooltip in `gtip.title`, placed by its bottom (above the control),
and the close says "Close"; the close's art rests in `icon.tint` (with `close.rest` equal to it),
takes `close.hot` under the pointer and goes back on leave; and Azeroth's colors differ from Ka0s
Default's, so the second skin pins something the first does not. The colors are read off the kit's
paint recorder (`tests/_kit/mock_paint.lua`). Run against the first-cut library files, four of the
five go red: both tooltip cases (no tooltip on the close) and Azeroth's color case (its close rested in
its old gold, `#F5C542`).

**Left as it is: the drag strip's tooltip placement.** The strip's tooltip opens beside the strip
through this addon's `tooltipPlace` hook (`modules/Anchors_Tooltip.lua`), which was the owner's ruling
on issue #22. The re-cut moves only a strip with no hook above it. Moving this one is the owner's call,
so it is a smoke line here, not a change.
